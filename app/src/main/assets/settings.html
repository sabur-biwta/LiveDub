AI translators

Language and voice

Male and female voices

With "Auto" the extension listens to the speaker for about half a second at the start of each sentence, measures the voice pitch, and answers in a male or female voice to match.

Gemini voice

Uses the API key of your Gemini translator above. A free key from Google AI Studio works, within Google's free daily limits; when a limit is reached the Google voice is used for a while.

 
Browser / Edge voice

Microsoft Edge has natural Bangla voices (Pradeep - male, Nabanita - female). They are picked automatically; change them here if needed. Chrome usually has no Bangla voice.
